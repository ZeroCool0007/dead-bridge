# Dead Bridge

A 3D zombie defense game that runs in your phone's browser.

**Play:** https://zerocool0007.github.io/dead-bridge/

You're holding the last bridge out of a fallen city. Survive the waves, earn coins and unlock 16 guns. A giant mutant walks out of the horde every 2 minutes, and vampire bunnies and grave ghouls join the fight from wave 3.

## Controls
- **Phone:** hold your finger on the soldier to shoot, and slide to move. Tap the 💣 button for a bomb.
- **Computer:** hold **A** to shoot, use the arrow keys to move, Space for a bomb, R to reload.

Add it to your home screen to play it like an app. It needs an internet connection the first time it loads.
